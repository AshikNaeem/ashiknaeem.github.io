Introduction

I am M Ashikul Haque Naeem, working as a Research Fellow in the Department of Nanomaterials and Ceramic Engineering at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), performing research in the area of nanoscale electronic devices and semiconductor materials. I am currently pursuing M.Sc. in Nanomaterials and Ceramic Engineering at BUET and completed my B.Sc. in Glass and Ceramic Engineering from the Rajshahi University of Engineering and Technology in 2024.

Prior to joining BUET, I worked as a Research Assistant at the Bangladesh Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (BCSIR) from 2024 to 2025 and at the Department of Materials Science and Engineering, University of Rajshahi, from 2023 to 2024. My current research interests include optoelectronic devices, semiconductor materials, and nanoscale electronic systems.
